# Gyan Dairy Sales Excellence Program – Allahabad & Varanasi (2022)

**Operational Deep-Dive & Market Intelligence Project**  
**Role**: Project Coordinator

![Project Banner](images/banner.png)

## 📋 Project Overview

Played a key role in a strategic **Sales Excellence Initiative** for Gyan Dairy in collaboration with BOD Consulting. Supported Phase 1 (8-week Operational Deep-Dive) to diagnose sales challenges and build a foundation for aggressive growth in Allahabad & Varanasi markets.

**Business Goals**:
- Varanasi: Double sales (~1,500 → 3,000 crates/day)
- Allahabad: 50% sales growth (~1,000 → 1,500 crates/day)

## 🗓️ Week-by-Week Execution

**Phase 1 (8 Weeks)** – See detailed table in `docs/week-by-week-plan.md`

- **Weeks 1-2**: Setup, target alignment & secondary research
- **Weeks 3-6**: Field interviews, retail mapping & data analysis
- **Weeks 7-8**: Insights synthesis, prioritization & final recommendations

## 📊 Key Deliverables & Results

- Comprehensive channel & retail mapping (Top 80% universe)
- Detailed gap analysis in visibility, availability & schemes
- Prioritized list of 5 Wave-1 initiatives
- Digital tracking dashboard implementation
- Roadmap for Phase 2 (12-month implementation)

## 🛠️ Technology & Tools Used

- Digital data collection & dashboards
- Excel + Python for sales data analysis
- Geo-mapping & heatmaps for market coverage
- Structured interview & reporting systems
